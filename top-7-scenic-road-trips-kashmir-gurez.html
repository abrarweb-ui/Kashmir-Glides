Top 7 Scenic Road Trips in Kashmir

Home›Blog›Kashmir Road Trips

Kashmir's true beauty unfolds along its winding mountain roads, roaring rivers, and dramatic high-altitude passes. Here are the top 7 road trips every travel enthusiast should experience.

1. Srinagar to Tangmarg & Gulmarg

A classic gateway drive passing through vibrant poplar avenues and rolling orchards before climbing up the winding green curves of Tangmarg.

2. Srinagar to Gurez Valley via Razdan Pass

An offbeat paradise drive featuring rugged mountain terrain, deep gorges, and unmatched solitude.

3. Srinagar to Pahalgam via Anantnag Willow Groves

A smooth highway drive flanked by mustard fields, saffron farms in Pampore, and the gushing Lidder River.

4. Srinagar to Sonamarg & Zojila Pass

A dramatic transition from lush green meadows to barren, snow-capped Himalayan heights.

5. Tangmarg to Drung Waterfall Route

A short, forest-heavy scenic detour leading to hidden streams and majestic waterfalls[cite: 1].

6. Mughal Road (Shopian to Bafliaz)

Historic route steeped in history, offering sweeping panoramic views of Pir Panjal mountain ranges.

7. Wular Lake Loop Road

A peaceful shoreline drive around one of Asia's largest freshwater lakes, perfect for sunset views.
