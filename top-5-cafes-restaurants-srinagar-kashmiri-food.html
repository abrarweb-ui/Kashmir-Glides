Top 5 Cafes & Restaurants in Srinagar

Home›Blog›Srinagar Food Guide

Srinagar's culinary scene is a rich blend of centuries-old royal traditions and cozy modern coffee culture. Here are the top 5 dining spots you must check out.

1. Mughal Darbar

The gold standard for authentic multi-course Wazwan feasts, Rogan Josh, and Tabak Maaz in the heart of Srinagar.

2. Ahdoos Hotel & Restaurant

Operating since the 1910s, Ahdoos is legendary for its traditional bakery items and deeply satisfying local meat preparations.

3. 14th Avenue Cafe & Tea Room

A gorgeous riverside cafe overlooking the Jhelum River, offering fantastic continental food, local brews, and desserts.

4. Lhasa Restaurant

Located near Boulevard Road, this spot is famous for incredible Tibetan and Chinese cuisine alongside local culinary options.

5. Stream Restaurant

Designed with water streams running right through the dining floor, offering an immersive ambiance paired with great food.
