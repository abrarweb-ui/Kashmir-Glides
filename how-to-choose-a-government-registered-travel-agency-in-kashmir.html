Why Registration Matters

Kashmir tourism has grown fast, and along with legitimate operators, a lot of unregistered agents now advertise on social media and messaging apps. A government registration with the Department of Tourism, Jammu & Kashmir isn't a formality — it means the agency is accountable, traceable, and operating within the state's tourism regulations. It's the first thing to check before you send any advance payment.

A 6-Point Verification Checklist

  • Ask for the exact registration number (format like JKEA00005750) and its validity date.
  • Check that the agency has a real, listed office address — not just a WhatsApp number.
  • Look for a working website with a proper "About" page stating registration details.
  • Confirm they don't demand full advance payment before sharing a written itinerary.
  • Check for verifiable reviews across multiple platforms, not just testimonials on their own site.
  • Ask whether vehicles are insured and GPS-tracked — a sign of an operationally serious agency.

Red Flags to Watch For

Be cautious of agents who insist on full payment upfront with no written confirmation, quote prices dramatically below every other agency (usually a sign of hidden add-on charges later), operate only through personal WhatsApp numbers with no company name, or can't produce a registration number when asked directly.
