Top 5 Luxury Hotels & Resorts in Gulmarg

Home›Blog›Gulmarg Luxury Hotels

Gulmarg is famous for its snow-covered slopes and world-class skiing, but it is also home to some of the most luxurious mountain resorts in India. Whether you are looking for heritage charm or modern 5-star amenities, here are the top 5 luxury properties to elevate your stay.

1. The Khyber Himalayan Resort & Spa

Perched on a ridge overlooking the Affarwat peaks, Khyber is synonymous with absolute luxury. Featuring Kashmiri architecture, heated indoor pools, and world-class dining, it sits right next to the Gulmarg Gondola.

2. Kolahoi Green Heights

Offering exceptional comfort and warmth, Kolahoi Green Heights provides splendid views of the valley, cozy wooden interiors, and top-tier hospitality tailored for families and couples.

3. The Vintage Gulmarg

A blend of classic alpine aesthetics and contemporary luxury. Known for its spacious rooms, grand fireplaces, and remarkable multi-cuisine dining options.

4. Grand Mumtaz Resorts

Set amidst pine trees, Grand Mumtaz offers a lavish retreat with traditional Kashmiri decor, excellent heating facilities, and serene natural surroundings.

5. Nedous Hotel Gulmarg

A historic landmark hotel that has hosted royalty and literary giants for decades. It delivers an old-world colonial charm matched with premium modern service.
