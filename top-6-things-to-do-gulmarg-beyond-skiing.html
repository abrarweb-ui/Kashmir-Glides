Top 6 Things to Do in Gulmarg Beyond Skiing

Home›Blog›Gulmarg Beyond Skiing

While the Gulmarg Gondola and winter skiing get all the headlines, Gulmarg offers an incredible array of activities for nature lovers and explorers throughout the year.

1. Explore the Secret Strawberry Valley

A gorgeous pocket surrounded by rolling green hills where wild strawberries grow during summer. Perfect for quiet walks and cinematic photography.

2. Play a Round at Gulmarg Golf Course

One of the highest green golf courses in the world, offering a challenging layout surrounded by towering pine trees and snowy summits.

3. Visit St. Mary’s Church

A Victorian-era British church built in the early 1900s, known for its distinct wooden architecture and peaceful location.

4. Trek to Alpather Lake

A high-altitude alpine lake nestled right under the twin peaks of Apharwat, partially frozen even during early summer.

5. Experience ATV Rides Through Outer Meadows

Take an exhilarating all-terrain vehicle ride through rough forest tracks and open meadow paths.

6. Birdwatching and Nature Photography

The Biosphere Reserve near Gulmarg houses rare Himalayan flora and fauna, making it a dream destination for wildlife enthusiasts.
