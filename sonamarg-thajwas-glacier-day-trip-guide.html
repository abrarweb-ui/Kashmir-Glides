Sonamarg Day Trip Guide: Exploring Thajwas Glacier & Meadow of Gold

Home›Blog›Sonamarg Guide

Translating literally to the **"Meadow of Gold,"** Sonamarg is where golden sunlight strikes snow-clad mountain peaks, creating an ethereal landscape. Located along the Srinagar-Leh highway, it serves as a premier destination for day excursions and glacier trekking.

Highlights of Sonamarg

  • Thajwas Glacier: Accessible via short pony ride or trek, offering year-round snow play right amid alpine meadows.
  • Sindh River: Flowing parallel to the valley, offering pristine spots for relaxation and photography.
  • Gateway to Ladakh: The starting point for high-altitude expeditions toward Zojila Pass.
