No journey to the valley is complete without experiencing authentic **Kashmiri cuisine**. Infused with aromatic spices like saffron, fennel, and cardamom, traditional dishes reflect the profound cultural heritage of the region.

Must-Try Kashmiri Delicacies

  • Wazwan Feast: A traditional multi-course meal featuring iconic dishes like *Rogan Josh*, *Rista*, and *Gushtaba*.
  • Kahwa: Traditional green tea brewed with saffron, almonds, and cinnamon, served warm.
  • Modur Pulao: Sweet saffron rice enriched with ghee, dry fruits, and nuts.
