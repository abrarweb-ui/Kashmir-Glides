Pahalgam Valley Tour Guide: Lidder River, Baisaran & Mini Switzerland

Home›Blog›Pahalgam Guide

Known affectionately as the **Valley of Shepherds**, Pahalgam is a pristine Himalayan town set along the rushing turquoise waters of the Lidder River. Whether you are traveling with family or looking for alpine trekking trails, Pahalgam offers an immersive encounter with raw nature.

Top Attractions You Cannot Miss

  • Baisaran Meadow ("Mini Switzerland"): Surrounded by dense pine forests and snow-capped peaks, this gorgeous meadow is accessible via pony ride or light trek.
  • Betaab Valley: Named after the Bollywood film shot here, it features vast green lawns, gushing streams, and willow trees.
  • Aru Valley: A peaceful village serving as the base camp for several high-altitude Himalayan treks.

Experience River Rafting on the Lidder

For adventure seekers, the rapids of the Lidder River offer thrilling white-water rafting experiences suitable for both beginners and experienced paddlers, managed safely by certified local instructors.
